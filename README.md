# Five Seconds to Explain the Glass

A house cat's official statement, delivered within five seconds of knocking a glass off the table: the glass had been squatting in the cat's sunbeam for three hours, so it was evicted, the shattering was the glass's own fault, and the cat would do it again. No apology, no regret — just a cat defending its territory.

> That glass was squatting in my sunbeam for three hours, so I evicted it, and the shattering was its own fault; I would do it again.

`main.py` writes this single sentence to `/output/entry.txt`.
